# Initial Mailbox Findings

## Funding records are fragmented

The mailbox contains separate evidence for Cisco/SVCF, QTS, and a16z funding. Award letters, payment threads, supporting nonprofit documents, budgets, and program evidence are spread across multiple conversations. The pipeline consolidates them by funder and status instead of treating every amount as received revenue.

## The data center curriculum has version drift

At least these variants should be compared and resolved into one canonical set:

- `AARI_Data_Centers_Cohort_Curriculum_v2_3 (1).docx`
- `AARI_Data_Centers_Cohort_Curriculum_v2_2-2.pdf`
- `AARI_Data_Center_Curriculum_Microsoft_Proposal.pdf`
- `Proposal_ AARI Data Center Curriculum (1).pdf`
- Four role-specific data center curricula dated June 29, 2026

The ML report should identify duplicated modules, missing prerequisites, conflicting sequencing, and the best canonical source.

## Governance and grant compliance need one checklist

The mailbox contains nonprofit articles, IRS confirmation materials, and grant requests that reference a determination letter, Form W-9, Form 990, and an audit if available. These should become a single compliance register with document status, owner, and renewal/reporting dates.

## Operational evidence is strong but scattered

The record set includes a completed colocation agreement, data center build plan, budget, Cisco hardware wishlist, Microsoft/ATC proposal, Security+ SOW, Splunk lab materials, student assignment evidence, and a thermal intelligence architecture. Together, these document an operating system, but they are not currently connected by a shared action register or architecture map.

## Restricted records must stay outside the general AI corpus

Student resumes, signed cohort agreements, W-9s, invoices, remittance notices, signature audit trails, and unrelated equity/legal records were found. They are separated into `99_restricted` and marked `index_allowed=false` by default.
