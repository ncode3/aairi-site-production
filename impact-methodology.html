Impact methodology

How AARI Tracks Impact

AARI uses internal program records, completed event documentation, partner conversations, and outcome confirmations to track current impact indicators.

Raised to date

Based on committed funding records, including corporate, grant, and philanthropic support.

Students trained

Based on internal attendance and cohort records.

Workshops

Based on completed AARI events and partner programming.

Partners engaged

Based on active conversations, confirmed collaborations, or program support.

Placement outcomes

Based on student-reported or partner-confirmed outcomes.

Infrastructure footprint

Based on current and planned facility/campus infrastructure work.

AARI is continuing to formalize annual reporting and public impact documentation as programs scale.
