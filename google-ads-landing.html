Building Atlanta’s AI Infrastructure Workforce Pipeline

AARI supports practical education in AI infrastructure, robotics, cloud systems, edge computing, and related technical pathways for Atlanta students and learners.

Support AI infrastructure education in Atlanta

What We Do

AARI develops hands-on learning experiences around AI infrastructure, robotics, cloud systems, edge devices, technical operations, and workforce readiness.

Who We Serve

AARI serves students, emerging technical learners, educators, and community partners connected to Atlanta-based education and workforce pathways.

Programs

Programs include workshops, lab sessions, scholar-to-mentor activities, technical exposure events, infrastructure curriculum, and partner-supported learning opportunities.

Why It Matters

AI systems depend on skilled people who understand compute, networking, data center operations, robotics, and deployment. AARI helps learners build that foundation.

Support the Mission

Donations support nonprofit education, student programming, equipment, instruction, and operations.
